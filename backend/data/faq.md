# Marketplace Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I register as a supplier?
To register as a supplier, click the 'Become a Supplier' button on the homepage. You will need to provide your company registration number, GST details, and a primary contact email. Once submitted, our verification team will review your application within 2-3 business days.

## How does the RFQ (Request for Quotation) process work?
When a buyer submits an RFQ, it is matched with verified suppliers who offer the requested category of products. Suppliers receive an email notification and can submit their quotes directly through the platform dashboard. The buyer can then compare quotes and select the best offer.

## Is the platform free to use?
The platform is completely free for buyers. For suppliers, we charge a nominal 2% transaction fee on successfully completed orders. There are no registration or monthly subscription fees.

## What is a 'Verified Supplier'?
A verified supplier has undergone a background check by our team, confirming their business registration, tax details, and physical address. Verified suppliers rank higher in search results and have a trust badge on their profile.

## How do I handle shipping?
Currently, shipping is handled directly between the buyer and the supplier. The marketplace facilitates the RFQ and order agreement, but the logistical arrangement and costs must be discussed and finalized in the quote.
